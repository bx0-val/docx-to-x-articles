# Privacy policy: Docx to X Articles

Docx to X Articles does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- The Word document you drop is read and converted **entirely inside your browser**.
- Its text is written directly into the X Articles editor you have open, and its images are uploaded **only to X**, through X's own article editor, exactly as if you had added them yourself.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own, has no analytics, and contacts no servers other than the X page you are already using.
- It runs only on `x.com` and `twitter.com`, and only acts when you drop a `.docx` file on an article draft.

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository.
